# Study plan

## Main goal
Get better step by step at tournament and Sit & Go poker, starting with free games.

## Current focus (max 3)
1. Log every important hand from each session (to build data for reviews).
2. Learn the basic math: outs, pot odds, equity.
3. Know your stack size in big blinds at all times.
