# Knowledge

Short lessons in simple English, one topic per file. They also feed the app's Math School.

Rules:
- Every number in a lesson comes from the odds engine or a named source.
- Numbers not checked yet are marked **"to validate"**.
- Shortcuts (like the rule of 2 and 4) are marked as shortcuts.
