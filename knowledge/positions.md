# Positions

- Your position is where you sit compared to the dealer button. It decides when you act.
- Order before the flop: UTG (first to act), UTG+1, MP, HJ, CO (cutoff), BTN (button), SB, BB.
- After the flop the button acts last. Acting last is a big edge: you see what everyone did first.
- Play fewer hands from early positions, more from late positions (CO, BTN).
- The blinds play many pots out of position after the flop.
