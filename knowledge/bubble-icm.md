# The bubble and ICM (idea only)

- Bubble = just before the paid places.
- ICM (Independent Chip Model) turns chips into prize money. Chips you lose hurt more than chips you win help.
- Medium stacks: avoid risky calls near the bubble, especially vs bigger stacks.
- Big stacks: can pressure medium stacks.
- Very short stacks: still need to push sometimes.
- Any ICM number: **to validate** (needs an ICM calculator).
