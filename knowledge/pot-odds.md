# Pot odds

**Equity needed = your call ÷ (pot + your call).** "Pot" = everything in the middle now, including his bet.
If your equity is higher, the call makes money over time.

| Bet | Pot now | You call | Equity needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Half pot (200 → bet 100) | 300 | 100 | 25.00% |
| Full pot (200 → bet 200) | 400 | 200 | 33.33% |
| Double pot (200 → bet 400) | 600 | 400 | 40.00% |

Numbers from `node app/engine/cli.js potodds`.
