# Push or fold (idea only)

- With a short stack, a small raise then a fold wastes chips. So: all-in (push) or fold.
- Many players switch to push-or-fold around 10 to 15 big blinds: **rule of thumb, to validate**.
- Fewer players behind you = you can push more hands.
- Pushing first beats calling an all-in: others can fold and you win the blinds.
- Push/fold charts: **to validate**. We add them after checking a trusted source together.
