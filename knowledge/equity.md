# Equity basics

Equity = your share of the pot if all cards were dealt: wins plus half of split pots.

| Matchup (preflop) | Equity |
|---|---|
| QsQd vs AhKc | 57.16% vs 42.84% |
| 7s7d vs AhKc | 55.39% vs 44.61% |
| AhKc vs AdQs (dominated) | 74.02% vs 25.98% |
| AhKc vs 8s7s | 58.10% vs 41.90% |

All exact, 1,712,304 deals each, from `node app/engine/cli.js equity`. Other suits give slightly different numbers.
